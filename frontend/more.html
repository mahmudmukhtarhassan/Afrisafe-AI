About AfriSafe AI

AfriSafe AI is an intelligent healthcare platform designed to improve early disease detection and preventive healthcare across Africa. Our AI-powered malaria risk assessment helps users identify potential malaria risk based on symptoms and exposure factors, providing personalized recommendations for next steps.

Mission: To build Africa's most trusted AI-powered preventive healthcare platform, helping millions detect health risks early and make informed medical decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AfriSafe AI a medical diagnosis?

No. AfriSafe AI provides AI-assisted screening only. It is NOT a medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for proper testing and treatment.

How does the assessment work?

Our AI model analyzes your reported symptoms, exposure factors, and duration to estimate malaria risk probability using a logistic regression model.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted and stored securely. Only you can access your assessment history.

Terms & Privacy

By using AfriSafe AI, you agree that this application provides AI-assisted screening only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions about a medical condition.

Your personal data and assessment history are stored securely and are only accessible to you. We do not share your data with third parties.
