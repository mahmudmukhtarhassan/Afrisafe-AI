About AfriSafe AI
Learn about our mission
AfriSafe AI is an AI-powered malaria screening tool built for Nigeria. Our mission is to provide accessible, fast, and reliable health assessments to communities across Africa using machine learning and modern technology.

FAQ
Common questions
How does the AI assessment work?
The AI model analyzes your symptoms and risk factors to estimate malaria likelihood. It's trained on health data relevant to Nigerian contexts.

Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted and stored securely. Only you can access your assessment history.

Can I use this offline?
An internet connection is required for AI predictions, but your history is cached locally.
