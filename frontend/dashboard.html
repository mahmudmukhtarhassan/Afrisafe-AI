Insecticide-Treated Nets (LLINs)

Sleep under Long-Lasting Insecticide-Treated Nets every night. Ensure nets are tucked under mattress edges without tears.

Standing Water Drain & Vector Control

Inspect household surroundings weekly. Clear rain gutters, cover water drums, and fill open puddles where mosquitoes breed.

Test Before Treatment (RDT / Microscopy)

Never take antimalarials without a confirmed parasite test. Prompt rapid testing prevents drug resistance and misdiagnosis.
